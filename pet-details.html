Charlie is a brilliant and loyal companion who loves long hikes and playing fetch. He was rescued from a construction site and has since blossomed into a deeply loving dog looking for a forever family.

Charlie's Rescue Story

Charlie was found wandering near a busy highway interchange, scared and slightly malnourished. A kind commuter spotted him and used the Voice of Stray app to report his location. Our rescue team arrived within 30 minutes, using calm reassurance and treats to secure him.

Upon arrival at the shelter, Charlie was timid but showed no signs of aggression. He quickly warmed up to the staff, showing his true goofy and affectionate Golden Retriever nature. After two weeks in a loving temporary foster home, he has fully recovered, gained healthy weight, and learned basic commands like "sit" and "stay".

Adoption Requirements

To ensure Charlie finds his perfect forever home, the shelter has set the following requirements:

  • Family Type Great for active families, couples, or singles. Good with older children (7+).
  • Yard / Home Fenced yard required. Not highly suitable for small apartments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Charlie's adoption fee?

Charlie's adoption fee is $150. This covers his previous medical treatments, neutering, full vaccinations, and microchipping.

Can I foster Charlie before committing?

Yes! City Rescue NGO offers a 2-week "Foster-to-Adopt" trial period to ensure Charlie is the perfect fit for your lifestyle.

What is the return policy?

If things don't work out, Charlie MUST be returned directly to City Rescue NGO. We provide behavioral support to help overcome any initial adjustment challenges.

Will I get his medical records?

Yes, all verified medical history, vaccination certificates, and microchip transfer paperwork will be provided during the adoption handover.
