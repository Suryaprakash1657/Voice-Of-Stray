Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to volunteer?

Not at all! We have roles for all experience levels. While emergency rescue missions may require specialized training (which we provide), tasks like feeding drives, transportation, and fostering are perfect for beginners.

Can students volunteer?

Yes, students over the age of 16 can volunteer for most tasks. Those under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian during feeding drives and community events.

Is there any remote volunteering available?

Absolutely! We constantly need help with social media management, helpline coordination, graphic design, and grant writing. You can help save lives right from your laptop.

Can I volunteer weekends only?

Yes. When you set up your Individual Rescuer profile, you can set your exact availability zone and schedule. You will only be pinged for opportunities that match your settings.

Are rescues safe?

Safety is our top priority. We provide comprehensive safety guidelines, mandatory training for field roles, and you will always be paired with an experienced rescuer or verified NGO on your first few missions.
