Adoption FAQ

Everything you need to know about welcoming a new pet.

What are the standard adoption fees?

Adoption fees vary by NGO and the animal's medical history. Generally, fees cover vaccinations, spay/neuter surgery, and microchipping. They typically range from $50 to $200.

Can I foster a pet before fully adopting?

Yes! We highly encourage our "Foster-to-Adopt" program. It allows you to take a pet home for 1-2 weeks to ensure they are the right fit for your family and lifestyle before making a final commitment.

What is the return policy if things don't work out?

We understand that sometimes things don't work out. All of our NGO partners require that the pet be returned directly to them. We offer support and behavioral resources first to help resolve any issues.

How long does the approval timeline take?

Most applications are reviewed within 48-72 hours. The entire process from application to bringing your pet home usually takes about 1 to 2 weeks, depending on the required home checks and meet-and-greets.

Will I receive the pet's full medical history?

Absolutely. Transparency is our priority. You will receive a complete packet of the pet's known medical history, vaccination records, spay/neuter certificates, and any ongoing medical needs upon adoption.

Can't adopt right now?

There are many other crucial ways to support our mission and help animals in need.
